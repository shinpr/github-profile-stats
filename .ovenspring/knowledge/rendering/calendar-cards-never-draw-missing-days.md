# Signal Field and Activity Consistency draw complete calendars, including zero-contribution days

The GitHub collector requires dates in chronological order within each response window and checks the order and coverage of the stitched period (`decodeWindow` and `combineWindows` in `src/github/contributions.mjs`). A complete series passed directly to snapshot normalization can arrive out of order. The normalizer sorts the entries and checks that they cover every date exactly once. A missing date prevents generation even when a duplicate keeps the entry count correct (`validateDailySeries` in `src/domain/snapshot.mjs`).

Applies to changes that distinguish quiet days or periods from missing data. A complete zero-contribution period is valid and draws a cell in the lowest activity color for every day. The undrawn spaces outside the displayed dates are week padding (`positionCalendarDays` in `src/render/calendar-grid.mjs`). A blank zero-day cell would look like that padding. Revisit the date and cell behavior if the collector, normalizer, or grid starts accepting gaps.
